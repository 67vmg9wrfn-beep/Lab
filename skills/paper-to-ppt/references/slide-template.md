# Per-Slide Template

Use this exact template when writing `<paper_id>.slides.md`.

## Slide 1: Title and Citation

- Paper title
- Authors, venue, year
- One-sentence paper claim

## Slide 2: Why This Problem Matters

- Practical or scientific importance
- Current gap in prior methods

## Slide 3: Research Question and Hypothesis

- Core question
- Stated or implied hypothesis

## Slide 4: Method Overview

- Pipeline in 3-5 steps
- Key modeling idea
- Diagram placeholder: `[Insert method diagram]`

## Slide 5: Data and Setup

- Dataset(s)
- Baselines
- Metrics
- Training/evaluation protocol

## Slide 6: Core Results

- 2-4 headline numbers
- Comparison vs strongest baseline
- Figure/table placeholder

## Slide 7: Ablation or Deeper Analysis

- Most informative ablation
- What it proves

## Slide 8: Strengths

- Top 3 strengths with evidence

## Slide 9: Limitations and Risks

- Top 3 limitations
- Generalization or bias risks

## Slide 10: Practical Implications

- Where it can be used
- Expected cost/complexity tradeoffs

## Slide 11: Final Takeaway

- Three-line summary:
  - Problem
  - Evidence
  - Decision recommendation

## Slide 12: Backup

- Extra experiment details
- Definitions and assumptions
